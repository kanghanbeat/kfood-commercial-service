# Sprint 3 Capital Region Food Draft

Status: Draft for user review  
Date: 2026-06-10  
Scope: Seoul, Gyeonggi, Incheon metropolitan launch content

## Executive Summary

Sprint 3 will prepare the first real content direction for the service. The
launch geography is the capital region, starting with Seoul plus nearby
Gyeonggi/Incheon cities that foreign travelers may reasonably visit by subway,
rail, bus, or short day trip.

This document is not production content yet. It is a research and review list.
The user will verify reliability before any item becomes published seed data.

## Content Principles

- Prefer foods that help travelers decide where to go.
- Prefer city/area associations that are commonly understood or easy to
  explain.
- Avoid claiming a dish is the exclusive origin food of a city unless verified.
- Label weak or market-based associations as `needs verification`.
- Keep the first production seed smaller than this list if verification takes
  too long.

## Facts vs Inferences

Facts with stronger current support:

- Uijeongbu is widely associated with budae-jjigae and budae-jjigae street.
- Incheon Chinatown has a strong historical association with jajangmyeon.
- Suwon galbi is a strong and recognizable city-food candidate.
- Seoul foods such as seolleongtang, tteokbokki, and market foods are useful
  for traveler-facing discovery, but specific neighborhood claims should still
  be checked.

Inferences requiring user verification:

- Anyang, Yongin, Gwangmyeong, and Ansan items are included as promising local
  market or city-route candidates, not as confirmed representative foods.
- Some Seoul associations are district or market associations rather than
  city-origin claims.
- The list is intentionally broader than the first production seed.

## User Verification Checklist

For each food, confirm:

- Is the city/area association reliable enough for public service content?
- Is the dish still relevant to travelers in 2026?
- Is the name understandable in English?
- Is there at least one official, tourism, city, market, or reputable source?
- Are there allergy/spicy/pork/beef/seafood cautions?
- Should the item be published, kept as draft, or removed?
